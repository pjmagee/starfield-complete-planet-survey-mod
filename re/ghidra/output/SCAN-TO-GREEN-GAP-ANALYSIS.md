## 1. Executive Summary (5 lines)

1. **No — we do not yet understand the full literal-scan-to-green path.** We have the *write* side (component-create → +0x24 canonical stamp → ID_52157/ID_52158 → +0x21) and the *read* side (ID_90491/ID_90548 → ID_52159(player, base+0x28)) decompiled, but we have **never empirically tied them together** with a single dump showing write-key == read-key.
2. The ONE thing most likely misunderstood: **the green render key is `vtable[0x228](instance)+0x28` (the BASE-FORM id, NO canonical step), while the write key is `ID_83006(base)+0x28` (canonical).** Green lights *only* when these are equal, i.e. iff `ID_83006(base) == base` (identity). We have been chasing "static vs materialization" — the wrong axis.
3. The canonical value itself is **asserted, never computed**: the two functions that actually *define* it — `ID_44957` and the `vtable[0x428]` callee inside `ID_83006` — are decompiled in **zero** dumps. No dump exhibits a single concrete species where authored (+0x20) ≠ canonical (+0x24).
4. Two ground-truth facts **outrank and partially contradict** the canonical theory: (G1) on-planet `CompleteSurvey` persists green while writing the **AUTHORED** id via `UpdatePlanetProgress`, and the engine's own cold-read aggregators (`ID_83011`/`ID_83013`) key by the **authored** id with identity-fallback. This strongly implies authored == canonical for at least flora, and that "materialization-only" is too pessimistic.
5. Two correctness landmines sit under everything: **all 80 dumps are 1.16.236; the shipped/played build is 1.16.244** (struct offsets unverified on the real binary), and the mod's `ID_126806` DbLookup is called 3-arg while the canonical-read sites (`ID_83009`, `ID_83038`) call it 6–7-arg — possibly a *different* lookup variant resolving a *different* table.
