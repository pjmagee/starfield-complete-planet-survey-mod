[b]Version 1.6.0[/b]

[list]
[*][b]New command: complete the whole star system.[/b] [font=Courier New]CompleteSystem[/font] finishes every planet and moon around the star you're in, with the same category choices as the other commands (resources, traits, fauna, flora, or all).
[*][b]Safer on game patches and missing Address Library.[/b] If critical offsets can't be resolved, the mod turns itself off with a clear notice instead of crashing the game.
[*][b]Safer while a long run is already going.[/b] Starting another completion mid-sweep is refused cleanly — no corrupted half-runs.
[*][b]Loads on more verified game builds.[/b] Declares Starfield 1.16.236, 1.16.242, and 1.16.244 (same layouts verified for this DLL).
[*][b]Orbital Scanner only fires from the star map.[/b] Avoids false auto-complete during land/load transitions.
[*][b]Clearer player messages.[/b] Short plain-English results and errors. Ship builds use a brief toast (not a big DEBUG dialog); full detail stays in the SFSE log.
[*][b]DLL + scripts update.[/b] ESM and Settings toggle FormIDs unchanged — your saves and toggle choices carry over.
[/list]

Built and tested on Starfield 1.16.244 / SFSE 0.2.21 (layouts also verified for 1.16.236 and 1.16.242).

[b]Version 1.5.0[/b]

[list]
[*][b]DLC and Creations worlds are now supported.[/b] The mod reads every plugin in your load order, not just Starfield.esm — so Shattered Space's Va'ruun'kai (Dazra) and any other DLC or mod-added planet can now be surveyed. This fixes the reported "not working on Dazra" bug: the Hand Scanner, the Orbital Scanner and the console commands all complete DLC worlds now (verified in-game on Va'ruun'kai).
[*][b]The galaxy-wide commands treat DLC life worlds correctly.[/b] Before, worlds like Va'ruun'kai could be mistaken for barren and marked "surveyed" while their flora and fauna were never actually scanned. They're now recognised as living worlds, same as base-game ones.
[*][b]DLL-only update.[/b] The ESM and scripts are unchanged — your saves and toggle settings carry over as-is.
[/list]

Built and tested on Starfield 1.16.244 / SFSE 0.2.21.

[b]Version 1.4.0[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Complete a survey straight from the galaxy map.[/b] New [b]Orbital Scanner[/b] toggle (Settings, Gameplay): with it on, scan a planet or moon on the star map and its entire survey completes for that body — resources, traits, flora and fauna — no landing required. Off by default, so the normal scan is unchanged unless you enable it.
[*][b]The star-map panel updates on the spot.[/b] After an Orbital Scanner scan the info panel jumps to 100% in place — no need to deselect and reselect the planet.
[*][b]The two auto-complete toggles were renamed to match.[/b] "Auto-Complete Survey on Scan" is now [b]Hand Scanner[/b], alongside the new [b]Orbital Scanner[/b]. Display names only — your saved on/off choices are unchanged.
[/list]

Built and tested on Starfield 1.16.244 / SFSE 0.2.21.

[b]Version 1.3.0[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Your character stats now keep up.[/b] Completing worlds updates the counters on your character sheet (Data, Statistics): Flora Fully Scanned, Fauna Fully Scanned, Unique Creatures Scanned, and Planets Scanned.
[*][b]Commands survive a trip to the Main Menu.[/b] Quit to the title screen, load a save, and the console commands still work. Before, they stopped being recognised until you restarted the game.
[*][b]One command finishes every living world.[/b] The living-world and whole-galaxy commands now complete everything in a single run, instead of leaving a few worlds just short of 100% until you ran it again.
[*][b]Running a command again does nothing extra.[/b] Repeat runs no longer re-count worlds that were already done, and the popup tells you when the galaxy is already fully surveyed.
[*][b]Adjust the counters yourself if you want.[/b] These are ordinary misc stats, so you can read or change any of them from the console with GetPCMiscStat and ModPCMiscStat.
[/list]

Built and tested on Starfield 1.16.244 / SFSE 0.2.21.

[b]Version 1.2.0[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Point each command at exactly what you want.[/b] Four commands (the world you're on, every dead world, every living world, or the whole galaxy). Each takes a category: resources, traits, fauna, flora, or all. Runs from anywhere.
[*][b]Every category sticks to its own job now.[/b] "fauna" doesn't grab nearby plants, "resources" doesn't do more than resources, "traits" only does traits.
[*][b]The survey finishes the moment the command runs.[/b] Survey panel, star map, and Survey Data all update right away, on any world, and it stays done after a reload.
[*][b]Creatures and plants next to you read as scanned once the area reloads.[/b] Already done when you land somewhere you finished from orbit, or after a quick trip to orbit and back on the world you're on.
[/list]

Built and tested on Starfield 1.16.244 / SFSE 0.2.21.
