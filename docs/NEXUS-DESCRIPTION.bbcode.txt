[size=6][b]Complete Planet Survey[/b][/size]

Finish planet surveys from the console. One world, or the whole galaxy.

Resources, traits, flora and fauna get marked surveyed. The survey hits 100%, the star map updates, and the [i]"<Planet> Survey Data"[/i] items land in your inventory. It works on worlds you've never even been to — including DLC and Creations worlds like Va'ruun'kai (v1.5.0+).

You pick which worlds and which categories to complete, so it fits how you play.


[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Starfield 1.16.244
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/106]SFSE[/url] 0.2.21
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/3256]Address Library for SFSE Plugins[/url]
[/list]

SFSE has to match your game build. If your game updates, wait for a matching SFSE and a new mod build before you update. The mod won't load on the wrong build.


[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]

Install with Mod Organizer 2 or Vortex ("Install from file"). Make sure [b]CompletePlanetSurvey.esm[/b] is ticked.


[size=4][b]How to use[/b][/size]

Open the console with the tilde key ([b]~[/b]) and run:

[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.<Command>" "<categories>"[/code]

[b]<categories>[/b] is any mix of [b]resources[/b], [b]traits[/b], [b]fauna[/b], [b]flora[/b], separated by commas. Or just [b]all[/b].

[size=3][b]The five commands[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]CompletePlanet[/b] - the world you're standing on
[*][b]CompleteSystem[/b] - every planet and moon around the star you're in (v1.6.0+)
[*][b]CompleteBarrenPlanets[/b] - every lifeless world (no plants or creatures)
[*][b]CompleteLifePlanets[/b] - every world with life
[*][b]CompleteAllPlanets[/b] - the whole galaxy, both at once
[/list]

CompleteSystem needs you near a planet or moon in that system (standing on one or in orbit). The galaxy commands run from anywhere. CompletePlanet needs you actually on a world.

[size=3][b]The categories[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]resources[/b] - surveys the world's resources
[*][b]traits[/b] - surveys the planet's traits
[*][b]flora[/b] - surveys the plants
[*][b]fauna[/b] - surveys the creatures
[*][b]all[/b] - all of the above
[/list]

Each one sticks to its own job. "fauna" won't touch plants. "resources" won't touch life. Combine them if you want. "resources,traits" does both. "fauna,flora" does the life.

[size=3][b]What to expect[/b][/size]

The survey finishes the moment the command runs. The survey panel and star map update straight away, the Survey Data drops into your inventory, and it stays done after you reload.

The creatures and plants right next to you read as scanned once the area reloads. Land on a world you finished from orbit and they're already scanned. On the world you're standing on, fly up to orbit and back and they catch up. Either way the survey is already complete.


[size=4][b]Play your way[/b][/size]

Same commands, different stories. Pick whatever fits your character.

[b]🧭 Explorer[/b] - "I survey each world myself as I land."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompletePlanet" "all"[/code]

[b]🛰️ Cartographer[/b] - "This whole system, then on to the next."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteSystem" "all"[/code]

[b]🚀 Frontier[/b] - "Chart the whole galaxy. All of it."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteAllPlanets" "all"[/code]

[b]🏔️ Adventurer[/b] - "Do the dead rocks for me. I'll walk the living worlds."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteBarrenPlanets" "all"[/code]

[b]🌿 Botanist[/b] - "Every plant in the galaxy. Nothing else."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteLifePlanets" "flora"[/code]

[b]⛏️ Miner[/b] - "Just what I can dig up. Resources everywhere."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteAllPlanets" "resources"[/code]

[b]🐾 Beast Master[/b] - "Every creature catalogued."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteLifePlanets" "fauna"[/code]

[b]🔭 Astrophysicist[/b] - "Every planetary trait, read from orbit."
[code]cgf "CompletePlanetSurveyQuest.CompleteAllPlanets" "traits"[/code]

Botanist and Beast Master use CompleteLifePlanets because plants and creatures only show up on living worlds, so it hits every one without bothering the dead rocks. Astrophysicist lines up with the Astrophysics skill, which reads traits from orbit.


[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]

Survey Data takes a minute or two to all show up. Finish a lot of worlds at once and the rewards trickle in, so the count keeps climbing after the command's done.

Stars don't have a survey, so a full all run lands around 1,700 items.

"resources" on its own leaves a living world's creatures untouched. That's the point, for a miner. Add fauna,flora or use all when you want the life too.

A dead world counts as done once its environment is surveyed, so it still gives you Survey Data even with no resources on it.


[size=4][b]Watch the XP[/b][/size]

Surveying gives XP, and finishing the whole galaxy in one go gives a pile of it. On a fresh character a single all run can shoot you up to around level 80, which makes the early game a walkover. Worth knowing before you pull the trigger.

If you'd rather skip the levels:

[list]
[*]Do it on a character that's already established, or run a handful of worlds at a time instead of everything at once.
[*]Pair this with a "no XP" or lower-XP-curve mod if you want the survey data without the levels at all.
[*]Already racked them up? You can set your level in the console with player.setlevel <number> (for example player.setlevel 5). Heads up: any console command turns achievements off for that save unless you're running an Achievement Enabler mod.
[/list]


[size=4][b]Optional settings[/b][/size]

Two toggles under Settings > Gameplay, both off by default:

[list]
[*][b]Hand Scanner[/b] - scanning any single plant or creature on foot finishes that whole world for you.
[*][b]Orbital Scanner[/b] - scanning a planet or moon on the star map finishes that world, and the info panel updates to 100% on the spot.
[/list]

Leave them off to do everything by command.


[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]

[b]Does it just paint the outlines green?[/b]
No. It writes the same underlying survey data a real scan writes — the green outline, the survey %, the scanner-panel details (genetics, reproduction, temperament), the Survey Data slate and the statistics all follow from that data. A world the mod completed is indistinguishable from one you surveyed by hand.

[b]Does it visit or load the planets in the background?[/b]
No. Nothing is visited, loaded, spawned or teleported. The mod reads each planet's authored contents from the game's own plugin files and writes the survey results into the same bookkeeping the game keeps in your save.

[b]Does it edit my save file?[/b]
No. It writes through the running game, using the engine's own functions; your next normal save stores the result exactly as if you had done the scans yourself.

[b]If I uninstall the mod, do the surveys revert?[/b]
No. Completed surveys are ordinary vanilla save data. Remove the mod and everything stays surveyed.

[b]Is the XP a cheat the mod adds?[/b]
The XP is the game's own reward for surveying — the mod completes lifeless worlds through the engine's own survey routine, and that routine grants the XP. The mod adds nothing on top. See "Watch the XP" above before running the whole galaxy.

[b]Does running a command twice double anything?[/b]
No. Re-runs detect the work is already done and do nothing — no double XP, no inflated statistics.

[b]Does it work with DLC and mod-added planets?[/b]
Yes (v1.5.0+). The mod reads your full load order — base game, Shattered Space, Creations, other mods — following the game's own load-order rules. No per-DLC patch needed.

[b]Does it conflict with other mods?[/b]
Very unlikely. The ESM only [i]adds[/i] two settings records and modifies no vanilla record. The DLL changes no game files on disk.

[b]Will it survive game updates?[/b]
Like every SFSE mod, the DLL needs a matching SFSE and Address Library for your exact game version — after a game patch, wait for those to update. Your completed surveys are never at risk; they live in the save.

[b]Do the console commands disable achievements?[/b]
Using the console flags the save as modded, as with any Starfield console command — that's the game's rule, not the mod's. An Achievement Enabler mod restores achievements if you want both.
